## Goal

User request (2026-11-08): over-the-air channels from the **HDHomeRun** tuner in Live TV, in a group named **Antenna** (user decision).

On the TV, when this is done:

- The guide's groups are **Favorites, My Teams, Antenna, All Channels**, then the playlist's groups. **Antenna** lists the tuner's channels by their real numbers (2.1, 2.2, 5.1 …), and **All Channels** lists the playlist's channels and then the antenna channels (user decision 2026-11-15).
- Antenna channels show their own number ("5.1") in the guide row, the banner and Search. They can be added to Favorites, hidden, moved, picked for Multiview and Search, and shown on Home's Live pages and in the muted home preview. Channel Up/Down zaps through them like any group.
- They have a **guide** from SiliconDust's free XMLTV service (2 days of listings; user decision 2026-11-15), so the guide grid, the banner's now/next, programme search and **My Teams** (a game on WSB or WAGA) work for them too.
- They start fast (the tuner answers in about 0.3 s) and 1080i channels are deinterlaced.
- The tuner has **2 tuners**. A third antenna stream (say a third antenna window in Multiview) shows **"Both antenna tuners are in use"** in its window instead of failing after a wait. A channel that can't play says why: **"No signal on this channel"**, "Both antenna tuners are in use" (another device has them), "The tuner is scanning for channels", "The antenna tuner isn't answering".
- **Settings → Live TV → Antenna**: Use Antenna on/off, the tuners found ("HDHomeRun CONNECT · 2 tuners · 185 channels"), **Find Tuners**, the guide (on/off and its status) and **Channels** (each channel Shown/Hidden, with its signal).
- Live TV works with only a tuner and no playlist (L13).

## Out of scope

- Starting a channel scan on the tuner (the HDHomeRun app does it; MMTVL only reads the lineup), L16.
- Channel logos for antenna channels (the lineup has none; the XMLTV `<icon>` could be used later). The guide and Live pages show the name, as for playlist channels without a logo.
- Recording / DVR (§2 non-goal). Tuner transcoding (EXTEND models only).
- ATSC 3.0 / DRM channels: the user's tuner is ATSC 1.0 (HDHR4-2US) with no DRM channels. DRM-tagged channels are left out of the lineup (L5); HEVC/AC-4 is untested.
- Choosing between several tuner devices per channel: with two devices carrying the same channel, the first device wins (L1). The user has one.
- An `Antenna` category in the casting or Audio & TV pages: none.

## Research & findings

### The user's tuner (read-only checks from the dev Mac, 2026-11-08 and 2026-11-15)

- **HDHomeRun CONNECT `HDHR4-2US`**, firmware `hdhomerun4_atsc` 20250623 (20261107 available), **TunerCount 2**, answers at `hdhomerun.local` (mDNS host name; there is **no** Bonjour service type: `dns-sd -B _services._dns-sd._udp` lists nothing HDHomeRun). `discover.json` keys: `FriendlyName`, `ModelNumber`, `FirmwareName`, `FirmwareVersion`, `DeviceID`, `DeviceAuth`, `UpgradeAvailable`, `BaseURL` (`http://<ip>:80`), `LineupURL` (`http://<ip>/lineup.json`), `TunerCount`. **`DeviceID`, `DeviceAuth` and the address are never logged, shown or committed** (master plan §6).
- **`lineup.json`**: 185 channels, keys `GuideNumber` (always), `GuideName` (always), `VideoCodec`, `AudioCodec`, `URL` (always `http://<ip>:5004/auto/v<GuideNumber>`), `HD` (38 channels), `Favorite` (6), `SignalStrength`/`SignalQuality` (only 104 of 185). 175 MPEG-2 + AC-3, 10 H.264 + AC-3, no DRM. **GuideNumbers are unique; GuideNames repeat** (`WYGA-CD` ×10, `WUVM-LP` ×6, `JTV` ×4, `ION`, `WXIA-TV`, `CREATE` … ×2). Majors 2…69, minors up to 12 (`26.12`), so numbers must sort numerically (2.2 < 2.10).
- **`lineup.m3u`** exists too (`channel-id="2.1" channel-number="2.1" tvg-name="WSB-HD" group-title="Favorites"`), but `lineup.json` carries more (codecs, signal) and is what the plan uses.
- **`lineup_status.json`**: `{"ScanInProgress":0,"ScanPossible":1,"Source":"Antenna","SourceList":["Antenna","Cable"]}`.
- **`status.json`** idle: `[{"Resource":"tuner0"},{"Resource":"tuner1"}]`; a tuner in use adds `VctNumber`, `VctName`, `Frequency`, `SignalStrengthPercent`, `SignalQualityPercent`, `SymbolQualityPercent`, `TargetIP` (**a LAN address: never logged**), `NetworkRate`. A tuner is busy when it has `VctNumber` (or any key beyond `Resource`).
- **Streams**: `GET <URL>` returns `200 video/mpeg` (MPEG-TS), **first byte in 0.26 s** (5.1, strong). 5.1 ran ~4.3 Mb/s over 3 s.
- **Errors (verified)**: a third stream with both tuners busy: `503`, `X-HDHomeRun-Error: 805 All Tuners In Use`, **in 7 ms**. An unknown channel: `404`, `801 Unknown Channel`. A channel with no signal (4.1): **`503` `807 No Video Data` after 10.0 s**. A weak H.264 channel (26.4, quality 71) played (200 in 0.27 s).
- **UDP discovery (verified)**: a discover request (below) sent to the **subnet-directed broadcast** address on UDP 65001 got replies from the tuner; sent to `255.255.255.255` it failed with `EHOSTUNREACH` (errno 65) on the dev Mac (several interfaces). The tuner sends **two replies** per request from the same address (one adds tag `0x2D`), so replies are de-duplicated by DeviceID. Reply tags seen: `0x01` device type (= 1, tuner), `0x02` DeviceID (4 bytes), `0x2B` DeviceAuth (24 bytes), `0x2A` BaseURL (20 bytes), `0x10` tuner count (1 byte, = 2), `0x27` LineupURL (32 bytes), `0x2D` multi type. CRC checked out.

### SiliconDust's documentation

- HTTP API ([info.hdhomerun.com/info/http_api](https://info.hdhomerun.com/info/http_api)): `discover.json`, `lineup.json` (+ `.xml`, `.m3u`); lineup `Tags` may hold `favorite` and `drm` (older firmware: `"DRM": 1`; handle both); stream URL `/auto/v<n>` picks a free tuner, `/tunerN/v<n>` a specific one; `?duration=<s>` closes after n s; the stream is MPEG-TS over HTTP and **the tuner is released when the client disconnects**. Status codes: 404 unknown channel; 503 busy / not found or not authorized within 5 s. `X-HDHomeRun-Error`: 801 Unknown Channel, 802 Unknown Transcode Profile, 803 System Busy (e.g. a channel scan), 804 Tuner In Use, 805 All Tuners In Use, 806 Tune Failed, 807 No Video Data, 808–810 DVR, 811 Content Protection Required.
- Discovery protocol (`libhdhomerun/hdhomerun_pkt.h`, [GitHub](https://github.com/Silicondust/libhdhomerun/blob/master/hdhomerun_pkt.h); libhdhomerun is LGPL 2.1: **MMTVL implements the documented packet format itself and copies no code**): UDP port **65001**; packet = `uint16 type`, `uint16 payload length`, payload, `uint32 CRC` (Ethernet CRC-32, **little-endian**; everything else big-endian). Payload = TLVs: `uint8 tag`, length (1 byte if ≤ 127, else 2 bytes: low 7 bits with the high bit set, then the rest `>> 7`), value. `HDHOMERUN_TYPE_DISCOVER_REQ 0x0002`, `RPY 0x0003`; tags `DEVICE_TYPE 0x01`, `DEVICE_ID 0x02`, `TUNER_COUNT 0x10`, `LINEUP_URL 0x27`, `BASE_URL 0x2A`, `DEVICE_AUTH_STR 0x2B`, `MULTI_TYPE 0x2D`; `DEVICE_TYPE_TUNER 0x00000001`, `WILDCARD 0xFFFFFFFF`.
  - The request MMTVL sends (computed with zlib's `crc32`): `00 02 00 0c 01 04 ff ff ff ff 02 04 ff ff ff ff 73 cc 7d 8f` (type 2, device type wildcard, device id wildcard, CRC).
  - A test reply vector (type 3; device type 1; id `0x1234ABCD`; tuner count 2; BaseURL `http://192.168.1.20:80`): `00 03 00 27 01 04 00 00 00 01 02 04 12 34 ab cd 10 01 02 2a 16 68 74 74 70 3a 2f 2f 31 39 32 2e 31 36 38 2e 31 2e 32 30 3a 38 30 7a f8 5b 30`.
- **XMLTV guide** ([Silicondust/documentation wiki: XMLTV Guide Data](https://github.com/Silicondust/documentation/wiki/XMLTV-Guide-Data), checked 2026-11-15): `GET https://api.hdhomerun.com/api/xmltv?DeviceAuth=<the DeviceAuth strings of all tuners, concatenated>`. **Two days of guide data for everyone, 14 days with an HDHomeRun DVR subscription** (history: "20260520 API is now available without a HDHomeRun DVR guide subscription"). DeviceAuth "changes regularly and is valid for 16-24 hours": **read it from the tuner(s) each time**. The client **must accept gzip**. Don't download at a fixed time: schedule the next download at a **random 20–28 hours** after the last. No numeric rate limit. (Alternative auth: DVR account email + DeviceIDs; not used.) [martin-rudy/hdhr-guide](https://github.com/martin-rudy/hdhr-guide) does the same (discover.json before every pull). Older docs said 3 days; the wiki is newer.
  - **Unverified:** the XMLTV's `<channel id>` and `<display-name>` contents (does a display-name carry the GuideNumber "2.1", the call sign, the GuideName?), whether `<icon>` is there, and the real days returned for this account. **Step 1 checks** (Q24).

### mpv (MPVKit 1.0.0 bundles mpv **0.41.0**, client API 2.5)

- `loadfile <url> [<flags> [<index> [<options>]]]`: since 0.38 the 3rd argument is an index, so per-file options need `["loadfile", url, "replace", "-1", "opt1=v1,opt2=v2"]`. **Per-file options are restored at the end of the file** ([input.rst v0.41.0](https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/blob/v0.41.0/DOCS/man/input.rst)), so an antenna channel's options never leak into the next IPTV stream on the same core.
- `--deinterlace=<yes|no|auto>` (default no): `auto` inserts a deinterlacer when ffmpeg flags the frame interlaced ([options.rst v0.41.0](https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/blob/v0.41.0/DOCS/man/options.rst)). For VideoToolbox frames `filters/f_auto_filters.c` (v0.41.0) has no hardware deinterlacer, so it **auto-converts to a software format and inserts `bwdif` (`mode=send_field`)**: 1080i30 becomes 60 fields-as-frames per second. **Unverified:** the CPU cost on the M4 with two 1080i windows in Multiview (Q25; checked in step 11). ATSC 1.0 is 1080i/720p MPEG-2: without deinterlacing it combs on motion.
- `MPVOptions.all` (`IPTV/Player/MPVOptions.swift:20-30`) is per core before `mpv_initialize`; `network-timeout=15` (above the tuner's 10 s no-signal answer, so mpv sees the 503).
- **Unverified:** that ffmpeg's `HTTP error 503 Service Unavailable` line reaches MMTVL through `mpv_request_log_messages("warn")` (`MPVHandle.swift:36`). ffmpeg's `http.c` logs it at warning level as far as known. Step 5 checks it on the dev Mac with channel 4.1 (no signal); without it the diagnosis still works from `status.json` (L9).

### Local decisions

- **L1 Ids and devices.** An antenna channel's id is `antenna:<GuideNumber>` (e.g. `antenna:5.1`): not tied to the device, so a replaced or re-addressed tuner keeps favorites, hiding, Multiview windows and recents. With several devices, devices are taken in DeviceID order and the first one carrying a GuideNumber wins (its `source` names that device). *Rationale:* GuideNumbers are unique per lineup; the user has one tuner.
- **L2 Channel fields.** `group = "Antenna"`, `name = GuideName` (empty → "Channel 5.1"), `tvgID = nil`, `tvgName = nil`, `logoURL = nil`, `guideNumber`, `source = .antenna(deviceID:)`, `streamURL` = the lineup's `URL`. `number` continues after the playlist's (`playlist.count + 1 …`) in GuideNumber order, so "Channel Up goes to the higher number" (`channelUpMovesUpList: false`) stays consistent. Shown numbers use `displayNumber`.
- **L3 Groups.** `ChannelLineup.groups` = Favorites (as today) + My Teams (as today) + **Antenna** (when a visible channel has `group == "Antenna"`) + **All Channels** (when the playlist's groups plus Antenna make more than one group) + the playlist's groups (first-appearance order, without "Antenna"). `channels(in: "Antenna")` = the visible channels whose group is "Antenna", **antenna channels first** (GuideNumber order), then a playlist group of that name if the playlist has one (a merge, documented). **All Channels = playlist channels, then antenna channels** (user decision): this falls out of `IPTVLibrary.channels = playlistChannels + antenna.channels`. Hiding the Antenna group (`hiddenGroups: ["Antenna"]`) hides them everywhere, All Channels included. `GuideModel.defaultGroupIndex`: the first group that is neither My Teams nor Antenna, else Antenna, else 0 (so the default stays Favorites / All Channels for this user).
- **L4 Discovery.** UDP to every interface's directed broadcast (255.255.255.255 fails on a multi-interface Mac), 1 s, de-duplicated by DeviceID; then `discover.json` per device. A device is kept only when: the reply's source is a LAN IPv4 (`LANAddress.isPrivate`), its `BaseURL` is `http` and its host **equals the reply's source address**, `TunerCount` is 1…16, and `discover.json` decodes. The `hdhomerun.local` fallback (used only when UDP finds nothing) requires `BaseURL`'s host to be a LAN IP literal. Devices live in memory only (no file, so no address or id on disk). Discovery runs at launch, on Find Tuners, after wake, and once when a lineup fetch fails. *Rationale:* the official, fast path; validation stops a LAN host from pointing MMTVL elsewhere.
- **L5 Lineup.** Fetched from `LineupURL` (which must have the same host as `BaseURL`) on the playlist's cadence: at launch, when Live TV opens if older than 10 minutes, the 15-minute timer and wake (D40), and Settings → Antenna → Find Tuners. An entry is kept when its `URL` is `http`, has the device's host, and isn't DRM (`Tags` contains `drm`, or `DRM == 1`); others are counted as `leftOut` (Settings says "N protected channels left out" when > 0). A failed fetch keeps the last channels (like a failed playlist re-fetch) and sets `status = .failed`. The tuner's own `Favorite` flags are ignored.
- **L6 Direct playback.** `play(url, direct: true)`: no `StreamResolver`, no `HLSRelay`, `loadfile <url> replace -1 deinterlace=auto`. Retries: a load that never reached `.playing` fails at once (`onFailure`, no retries, so L9 runs after one tuner answer); a stream that was playing and ends gets **one** reconnect after 2 s, then fails. `httpStatus` is parsed from mpv's log lines (`HTTP error (\d{3})`) since the last `loadfile`. The load log line says `"Loading a stream from the antenna tuner (player N)"`, never its address.
- **L7 Never touch a tuner ahead of time.** `prefetchNeighbors`, the guide's warm-up and the top shelf's stream prefetch skip channels with `isDirectStream` (filter before the URLs are built). Logos: none to fetch.
- **L8 Tuner budget.** `LiveTVModel` counts its own players that are playing or loading an antenna channel of a device (player 0 for the full-screen channel or the preview, plus `tilePlayers`). Starting another antenna stream on that device when the count is already `tunerCount` doesn't call `play`: full screen shows `AntennaProblem.allTunersInUse` as `playbackError` (can't happen with one stream, but kept for the rule); a Multiview window shows it as that window's message. A zap on the same player doesn't count twice.
- **L9 Diagnosis.** After an antenna player's `onFailure`: (a) the device's `status.json` (no tuner taken): every tuner busy → `.allTunersInUse` (another device has them); unreachable → rediscover + lineup refresh, then **retry once** if the channel is still there, else `.unreachable`; (b) `lineup_status.json` `ScanInProgress == 1` → `.scanning`; (c) `httpStatus == 404` → lineup refresh; retry once if the channel is still in it, else `.unknownChannel`; (d) `httpStatus == 503` (or nil) with a free tuner → `.noSignal`. Retry at most once per channel per open (the existing `refreshedAfterFailure` / `refreshedWindows` flags). The playlist is **not** re-fetched for antenna failures. Messages: `allTunersInUse(2)` "Both antenna tuners are in use" (other counts: "All N antenna tuners are in use"), `.scanning` "The tuner is scanning for channels", `.noSignal` "No signal on this channel", `.unknownChannel` "This channel is no longer on the tuner", `.unreachable` "The antenna tuner isn't answering", `.unknown` "Can't play this channel". The preview keeps its current behavior (ends, shows the art). *Rationale:* the tuner's own header isn't visible through mpv; status.json is free and answers the busy case at once.
- **L10 The antenna guide** (user decision: SiliconDust). One special guide source, `AntennaGuide.sourceID`, imported through `GuideImporter` with **only the antenna channels** and `AntennaGuide.match`. Its URL is built at import time from a **fresh** `discover.json` `DeviceAuth` of every device, concatenated in DeviceID order, put in the query with **every non-alphanumeric character percent-encoded** (`URLComponents` leaves `+` alone, which a server reads as a space): `https://api.hdhomerun.com/api/xmltv?DeviceAuth=<encoded>`. The token is never logged, stored (Keychain included) or shown; only `api.hdhomerun.com` is logged. Due: right away when antenna channels first exist and the guide has never imported; then **20–28 h (uniform random) after a success**, **1 h after a failure** (`iptv-state.json` `antennaGuideDue`). Refresh Now imports it only when the last import is more than 1 h old. `pruneUnusedGuides` keeps its rows; `removeSource` (which wipes `guide.sqlite`) sets it due now. Matching (`AntennaGuide.match`, antenna channels only, first rule that hits): 1. an override (`epg-overrides.json` key `antenna:<n>`, then the name; "" = no guide); 2. a `<display-name>` equal to the GuideNumber ("5.1"), or starting with the GuideNumber and a space ("5.1 WAGA"); 3. the normalized GuideName equal to a normalized display-name **only when exactly one antenna channel has that GuideName**. Step 1 may change rule 2's form to what the real file has, and records it here.
- **L11 Guide off.** With `antennaGuide: false`, MMTVL never calls SiliconDust, prunes the antenna guide's rows, and antenna channels are matched against the **pasted guides** with the existing `EPGMatcher` rules (name; tvg-id is nil), so Match Guide works for them.
- **L12 Match Guide on an antenna channel** offers the antenna guide's channels first, then the pasted guides', and writes the override under `channel.overrideKey` (`antenna:5.1`), removing any key equal to the id or the name first. `EPGMatcher` rule 1 checks `overrideKey` before the name. *Rationale:* GuideNames repeat on the tuner (WYGA-CD ×10).
- **L13 Antenna-only Live TV.** `IPTVLibrary.hasLiveSource` = `hasPlaylist || !antenna.devices.isEmpty`, and `canHaveLiveSource` = `hasPlaylist || antenna.isEnabled`. Live TV's setup screen shows only when neither a playlist nor an enabled antenna exists, or (antenna enabled, no playlist) after a discovery found no tuner: "Paste a playlist URL in Settings → Live TV, or connect an HDHomeRun tuner". The preview, the top shelf, the refresh gates and Settings' Live TV page use `hasLiveSource`. `.unavailable` for "no channels" says "No channels found" when only the antenna is set up.
- **L14 Settings.** `settings.json` **`antennaEnabled`** (default `true`; off: no discovery, no lineup, antenna channels gone) and **`antennaGuide`** (default `true`). Settings → Live TV gets an **Antenna** row in the Channels section (value: "1 Tuner · 185 Channels", "Searching…", "No Tuner Found", "Off"), shown with or without a playlist. The page `.antenna`: section *Antenna* (toggle **Use Antenna**); section *Tuners* (one info row per device: title FriendlyName, value "2 tuners", detail "<ModelNumber> · 185 channels"; then **Find Tuners**, detail = the probe line "Searching…" → "Found 1 tuner" / "No HDHomeRun found on this network"); section *Guide* (toggle **Guide from SiliconDust**, an info row with the status "Updated Nov 15, 9:12 AM · 1,234 programmes · 150 of 185 channels" / "Failed: …" / "Off"); section *Channels* (push **Channels**, value "185 Shown" / "12 Hidden"). The page `.antennaChannels`: one row per antenna channel, title "5.1  WAGA-HD", value "Shown"/"Hidden", detail "HD · Signal 100 %" (parts that exist), `isDimmed` when hidden, action `.toggleChannelHidden(id:)`. Footers and descriptions in step 9. Never a DeviceID, address or token.
- **L15 Logging.** Category **`Antenna`** (notice, kept by `log show`): "Found N tuners (M tuner slots)", "Loaded N antenna channels (K left out)", "Antenna lineup failed: <reason>", "Antenna guide from api.hdhomerun.com: …" (the importer's own line), "Antenna stream: <problem>", "Antenna tuners busy: n of m (MMTVL holds k)". Never DeviceID, DeviceAuth, an address, `TargetIP` or a stream URL. **All** mpv log lines are passed through `MPVLogText.redacted` (`https?://\S+` → `<url>`), which also protects the playlist's tokens.
- **L16 No channel scan** from MMTVL; Settings' Antenna footer says "To find new channels, scan in the HDHomeRun app."
- **L17** Remove Live TV Source removes the playlist and pasted guides only; antenna channels stay (they come from the tuner), and the antenna guide is due again.

## Risks & fallbacks

| Risk | Signal | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| The XMLTV doesn't carry GuideNumbers | Step 1 | Rule 3 (unique GuideName) + call-sign match; ask the user whether Match Guide by hand is acceptable for the rest. |
| SiliconDust withdraws the free tier or the token is refused (401/403) | Guide status "Failed: Server error (HTTP 403)" | The guide toggle off → pasted guides (L11). No retry storm: 1 h after a failure. |
| `bwdif` too heavy for two 1080i windows on the M4 | Stutter, high CPU in step 11.5 | Multiview tiles use `deinterlace=no` (full screen keeps `auto`), a per-file option set from the buffer profile. |
| mpv doesn't log the HTTP status | Step 5 | L9 already works without it (`status.json`; a free tuner → no signal). |
| The tuner doesn't free a tuner quickly after a zap | A zap in 2-antenna Multiview gets 805 | L9's busy diagnosis; retry once after 1 s when MMTVL itself held fewer than `tunerCount` streams. |
| The tuner's address changes (DHCP) | Lineup fetch fails | L4/L5 rediscover once; recommend a DHCP reservation in CLAUDE.md. |
| UDP broadcast blocked (Local Network denied) | "allow Local Network" log line; no tuner found | The `hdhomerun.local` fallback (mDNS; also needs Local Network). Settings → Live TV → Antenna says "No HDHomeRun found on this network". |
| A playlist group named "Antenna" | Two kinds of channel in one group | L3 merge (antenna first). |
